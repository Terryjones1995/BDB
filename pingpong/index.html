Break Room Ping Pong

BDB is the self-declared Break Room MVP. Fifteen minute break, one table, first to 11. Shut him up.

  • Move the mouse or your finger to move your paddle. It hits the ball when they meet.
  • Push up into the ball to hit harder. Too hard goes long.
  • Slide sideways through the hit for spin that curves the ball.
  • Tap to toss and serve. Serve flips every 2 points. Win by 2.
  • Space or P pauses.
