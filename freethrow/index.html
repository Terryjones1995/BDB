Free Throw Frenzy

Sixty seconds on BDB's own hoop, his face right on the glass. He averaged 2.0 a game. Make him feel it.

  • Drag up and let go to shoot. Point the flick at the hoop; a longer drag means more power.
  • Keyboard: hold Space to charge, let go to shoot. ← → aim, ↓ recentres.
  • 2 a bucket, 3 for a swish. Streaks multiply. Five straight and you're on fire.
  • The hoop starts moving at 30 seconds. The last 10 seconds are double points.
  • P pauses.
