Break Room Brawl

Over 2,400 cards in twelve types, and a ladder a thousand levels long. Build a 15 card deck, flood the lanes and knock down BDB's towers. Win coins, open packs, take his cards.

An account keeps your cards, coins and decks online, so you can sign in anywhere and your friends can see when you are on. Without one, everything saves on this device.

How coins work

Every win pays coins, and harder fights pay more. The first win on a ladder level pays in full, replays pay half, and a loss still pays a little. Spend coins on packs, decks, single cards, or upgrades for the cards you have. Every level adds 8% health and damage, up to level 10. A card you pull twice goes up a level for free.
