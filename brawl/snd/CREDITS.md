# Sound in Break Room Brawl

Two files: `sfx.mp3` holds every effect end to end (audio.js knows where each one starts), `battle.mp3` is the music.

## Music

"Battle Theme A" by cynicmusic, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain) at
https://opengameart.org/content/battle-theme-a. Edited here so it loops without a seam: the last chord's
ring is folded over the opening bars.

## Effects

Recordings by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released under CC0 1.0: Interface Sounds, RPG Audio, Casino Audio
and Music Jingles, plus four thuds and rumbles that reached this game through the Net Forge asset library
(CC0; it names Kenney's Impact Sounds, Sci-fi Sounds and Digital Audio as where its sounds come from).
Menus, cards, coins, chest lids, blades, blows and the losing jingle come from these, some of them
layered or re-pitched.

The fanfare for a win is from the Superpowers Medieval Fantasy asset pack by Pixel-boy for Sparklin Labs
(https://github.com/sparklinlabs/superpowers-asset-packs), released under CC0 1.0.
