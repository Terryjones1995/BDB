Where the models in this folder come from.

KayKit character packs by Kay Lousberg (kaylousberg.com), CC0 1.0 Universal:
  Adventurers, Skeletons, the Mystery Monthly characters and the Character Animations rigs.
  The characters share two clip libraries (am.glb, al.glb) and one weapon rack (wp.glb).
  Licence text: KayKit-Adventurers-LICENSE.txt, KayKit-Skeletons-LICENSE.txt.

Ultimate Monsters by Quaternius (quaternius.com), CC0 1.0 Universal:
  the Big_*, Blob_* and Flying_* files. Licence text: Quaternius-LICENSE.txt.

Made for this game with Hyper3D Rodin (generated, rigged and animated):
  Bellkeeper, Dragon, RedDragon, Troll, Orc, Ogre, Assassin, Golem, Wizard, Lich, Minotaur, Treant, Clockwork, Janitor.

Every file was cut down for the web: fewer triangles, small WebP textures, thinned keyframes, meshopt compression.
