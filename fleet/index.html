Sink BDB

BDB's navy is out there somewhere on a 10 by 10 grid, flying his face on every flag. Find it, sink it, and never let him forget it.

  • Drag your ships onto your grid. Tap a ship (or press R) to turn it. Then hit Ready.
  • Tap a square in his waters to fire. Arrows and Enter work too.
  • Sink all five before he sinks yours. Salvo: one shot per ship you've still got.
  • Wins score on accuracy, ships left afloat and BDB's level.
