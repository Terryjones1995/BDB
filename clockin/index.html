Clock-In Rush

BDB's shift starts at 11. It's 10:52 and he's still dribbling across the casino floor. Get him to the time clock.

  • Swipe down or Down to slide under the Employees Only signs.
  • Grab coffee for points. A lanyard takes one hit for you. The golden ball means a scout is watching.
  • Every 500 m he clocks in and the floor speeds up. P pauses.
